# Sources and their roles

| Source | Role in this package |
|---|---|
| Izgin, Kopecz, Meister, Schilling, *Numer. Algorithms* 96(3):1221–1242, 2024, https://doi.org/10.1007/s11075-024-01770-7; arXiv:2309.01562 | States the conjecture refuted here (abstract) and the desired global property (Section 1); its symmetric experiments are discussed in Section 1.2 of the paper. |
| Izgin, doctoral thesis, arXiv:2402.13788 | Lists the conjecture as open and of high importance (Chapter 6). |
| Izgin & Öffner, *ESAIM M2AN* 57(4), 2023 | MPDeC(8) only locally stable: the negative-tableau failure that prompted the conjecture. |
| Izgin, Kopecz, Meister, *ESAIM M2AN* 56(3), 2022 and *SIAM J. Numer. Anal.* 60(6), 2022; *PAMM* 22, 2023 | Local (centre-manifold) stability theory used for the local stability statements. |
| Burchard, Deleersnijder, Meister, *Appl. Numer. Math.* 47, 2003; Patankar 1980 | Origin of Patankar weighting and of modified Patankar schemes. |
| Kopecz & Meister, *Appl. Numer. Math.* 123, 2018; *BIT* 58, 2018 (arXiv:1703.05052) | Definition of MPRK22(alpha) and of the third-order MPRK43II(gamma) scheme. |
| PositiveIntegrators.jl, commit 37efc009f8baed988b999f56ee0dc76021bf8ee1 | Cross-check of the MPRK43II implementation only (not relicensed or included). |
| Huang & Shu, *J. Sci. Comput.* 78, 2019; Huang, Izgin, Kopecz, Meister, Shu, *ESAIM M2AN* 57(2), 2023 | SSP-MPRK schemes and their stability (exploratory comparison). |
| Öffner & Torlo, *Appl. Numer. Math.* 153, 2020; Bruggeman et al., *Appl. Numer. Math.* 57, 2007 | Related positivity-preserving schemes. |
| Torlo, Öffner, Ranocha, *Appl. Numer. Math.* 182, 2022; Izgin, Öffner, Torlo, SEMA SIMAI Springer Series, 2024 | Known oscillation and order-reduction issues of Patankar-type schemes. |
| Izgin & Ranocha, *J. Sci. Comput.* 109(3), 2026; Kelley & Keyes, *SIAM J. Numer. Anal.* 35, 1998 | Step-size control and pseudo-transient continuation, discussed in the section for users. |
| Iserles 1990; Hairer, Iserles, Sanz-Serna 1990; Iserles, Peplow, Stuart 1991; Yee, Sweby, Griffiths 1991; Humphries 1993; Stuart & Humphries 1996 | Classical theory of spurious numerical dynamics; positions the present mechanism. |
| Singer, *SIAM J. Appl. Math.* 35, 1978 | Schwarzian-derivative argument, discussed and shown not to apply directly. |

Full references with DOIs are in the paper's bibliography.
