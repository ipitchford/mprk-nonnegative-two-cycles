- 20 October 2026 (research stage): every bibliographic entry was checked
  against its Crossref record resolved from the DOI, or against arXiv
  metadata; the Stuart–Humphries monograph (no DOI) was checked against a
  Crossref-indexed review. An internal verification re-review independently
  resolved 23 DOIs and the thesis record, all matching.
- 20 October 2026 (publication intake): the 23 external DOIs in the paper were
  resolved again against Crossref; titles, volumes, pages and years agree with
  the bibliography. The Crossref and arXiv abstracts of the source paper were
  compared again; the paper's remark on their differences was completed
  accordingly (see PROVENANCE.md).
- The quotations from the source paper were compared with the arXiv full text
  (version 1) and the publisher's Crossref abstract; the journal full text was
  not retrieved automatically.

Bibliographic existence and agreement do not by themselves establish that each
source supports the sentence citing it; the source-to-claim roles are listed in
