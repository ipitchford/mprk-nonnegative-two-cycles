## 2026-10-26 — proof of Theorem 5 and §1.4 contribution 3

Source location: `paper.tex` lines 93 and 251.

Corrected two statements in the archived manuscript. (1) In the proof of Theorem 5, the chain $\sup_L|F'|\cdot\sup_H|F'|\le1.6823\cdot10^6\times5.333\cdot10^{-7}\le0.8971$ has a false last step: the product of the displayed factors is $0.89717\ldots$. The certified exact bounds in certificate C2 give $0.89706\ldots\le0.8971$, so the value 0.8971 in the proof and in Table 2, and the conclusion $<1$, stand. (2) Contribution 3 in §1.4 says the explicit parameter sets of Theorem 11 each cover all steps $K\ge10^4$. Only Theorem 11(a) does; Theorem 11(b) covers $K\in10^4[1-10^{-3},1+10^{-3}]$.

Neither correction changes a theorem or certificate. The archived PDF, version DOI and repository tag are unchanged; the erratum is recorded here and at <https://evidencepress.org/releases/mprk-nonnegative-two-cycles/>.
